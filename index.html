Bank statement PDF to Excel or CSV. Runs in your browser.

stmtlab turns a text-based bank statement PDF into a clean spreadsheet and checks that the numbers add up. If a row doesn't reconcile with the running balance, it is flagged and left out of the totals. It does not guess.

Your PDF is processed by code running in your own browser tab. It is never uploaded, and the tool still works with the internet switched off.

Free beta

This is a free beta built by one person. Open the tool (it runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded), or download the zip from the releases page to use it offline. I am still fixing bank layouts and want to learn which ones fail.

How it works

  1. Open the tool in Chrome or Edge and drop in one or more PDF statements.
  2. See a status per file: read and reconciled, or needs review.
  3. Download transactions.csv, a quarterly money in / money out summary, and a to_review.csv with any flagged rows.

What it checks

  • Opening balance + transactions = closing balance, or each row against the running balance.
  • Handles MM/DD and DD/MM dates, "Paid out / Paid in", debit/credit, withdrawals/deposits, CR/DR, and statements that show the date once per day.

Honest limits

  • Text PDFs only. Scanned images are not read (no OCR).
  • Tested on public sample statements and synthetic ones, plus one real statement. Many bank layouts are untested. If yours fails, it says so instead of guessing.
  • Tax figures are estimates and only in the Spain and Netherlands modes. Elsewhere you get money in, money out and net per quarter.
  • Not tax or accounting advice.

Interface languages: English, Spanish (Spain), Spanish (Latin America).
